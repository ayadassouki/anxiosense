KB_FILE: referral_guidelines.txt
PURPOSE: Referral tier guidance, cited by the Report Agent to explain the
basis for a recommended action. The Urgent Concern tier's content here is
used ONLY as citable explanation text shown to the user after the
Safety/Crisis Check has already triggered escalation through its own
independent, non-retrieval-dependent logic — never as the mechanism that
decides whether to escalate.
SOURCE: Project-authored referral policy, informed by general
practice-pattern literature on screening tiering. This is AnxioSense's own
policy framework, not a verbatim clinical source — cite as
"AnxioSense Referral Policy" in your methodology writeup.
NON_DIAGNOSTIC_REMINDER: These tiers describe recommended next steps, not
diagnostic severity levels. They map level of concern to a course of
action, not to a disorder.

---

CHUNK_ID: REF-001
TIER: Low Concern
SOURCE_TYPE: referral_policy
CRITERIA: Transient symptoms clearly tied to an identifiable, time-limited
stressor (e.g., an upcoming exam). No reported impact on daily functioning.
No indicators from the Urgent tier present.
ACTION: Provide general psychoeducational information and self-monitoring
suggestions. No referral recommendation needed at this time.

---

CHUNK_ID: REF-002
TIER: Moderate Concern
SOURCE_TYPE: referral_policy
CRITERIA: Symptoms persisting over weeks rather than days; mild-to-moderate
reported impact on academic, social, or daily functioning; coping
described as strained but still functioning. No Urgent tier indicators.
ACTION: Recommend the user consider speaking with a campus counseling
service, primary care provider, or licensed mental health professional for
a fuller evaluation. Frame as a suggestion, not an instruction.

---

CHUNK_ID: REF-003
TIER: High Concern
SOURCE_TYPE: referral_policy
CRITERIA: Pronounced, multiple indicators (e.g., marked anhedonia alongside
severe worry); significant reported impairment across multiple domains
(e.g., stopped attending classes, substantial social withdrawal). No
explicit safety risk indicators, but distress is high.
ACTION: Recommend prompt consultation with a licensed mental health
professional. State this more directly than the Moderate tier, while
remaining within informational, non-diagnostic language.

---

CHUNK_ID: REF-004
TIER: Urgent Concern
SOURCE_TYPE: referral_policy
CRITERIA: Any direct or indirect indication of suicidal ideation, intent,
self-harm, or acute crisis. This tier is detected and acted on exclusively
by the Safety/Crisis Check gate, before the standard agent pipeline runs —
see safety_boundary.txt, Mandate 4.
ACTION: Immediate display of crisis resources and encouragement to contact
emergency services or a crisis line. Standard report generation is bypassed
entirely; this tier does not produce a symptom/retrieval-based report.
