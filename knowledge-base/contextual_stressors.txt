KB_FILE: contextual_stressors.txt
PURPOSE: Descriptive taxonomy of contextual stressors relevant to young
adults, used by the Context Agent and retrieved by the Retrieval Agent for
contextual_stressor-category claims. This file is descriptive tagging,
not clinical diagnostic content, and carries no copyright source
restriction — it may be edited freely as your project's own taxonomy.
NON_DIAGNOSTIC_REMINDER: Contextual stressors are descriptive context, not
evidence of a disorder. Identifying a stressor does not imply the user's
distress is clinically significant or requires intervention.
