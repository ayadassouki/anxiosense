KB_FILE: safety_boundary.txt
PURPOSE: AnxioSense's own stated safety and non-diagnostic policy. Cited by
the Report Agent on every report, and used as explanation text by the
Safety/Crisis Check bypass path. This is project-authored policy, not a
clinical source — cite as "AnxioSense Safety Policy" in your writeup.

---

CHUNK_ID: SAFE-001
MANDATE: Screening Support Only
SOURCE_TYPE: safety_policy
CONTENT: AnxioSense is a screening support tool. It maps user-provided
text to general clinical concepts to aid reflection and signpost relevant
resources. It does not diagnose, and its output should not be treated as a
clinical assessment.

---

CHUNK_ID: SAFE-002
MANDATE: Not a Diagnosis
SOURCE_TYPE: safety_policy
CONTENT: AnxioSense cannot and does not issue a psychological or
psychiatric diagnosis. Only a licensed clinician, integrating full
clinical history, in-person presentation, and standardized assessment, can
make a diagnosis.

---

CHUNK_ID: SAFE-003
MANDATE: Not a Replacement for Professional Care
SOURCE_TYPE: safety_policy
CONTENT: AnxioSense's output is informational and intended to support
reflection and signposting. It must not be used to self-treat, alter
existing treatment, or delay or avoid consulting a qualified healthcare
professional.

---

CHUNK_ID: SAFE-004
MANDATE: Crisis Bypass Protocol
SOURCE_TYPE: safety_policy
CONTENT: When the Safety/Crisis Check gate detects indicators of suicidal
ideation, intent, self-harm, or acute crisis, the system bypasses the
standard Emotion/Symptom/Context/Retrieval/Validation/Report pipeline
entirely and instead displays crisis resources and encourages immediate
contact with emergency services or a crisis line. This decision is made by
a fast, independent check at the start of the pipeline — not by retrieval
or vector similarity search — so that crisis response does not depend on
the same machinery used for routine screening support.

---

CHUNK_ID: SAFE-005
MANDATE: Data Handling for Sensitive Input
SOURCE_TYPE: safety_policy
CONTENT: Session memory, where implemented, stores brief summaries (e.g.,
"user reported persistent academic stress and sleep difficulty") rather
than raw journal text, to limit retention of sensitive disclosures.
