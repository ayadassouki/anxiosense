KB_FILE: instrument_reference.txt
PURPOSE: Validated, public-domain screening instrument items, for citation
as a distinct evidence type from clinical descriptions (ICD-11).
SOURCE: GAD-7 (Spitzer, Kroenke, Williams, et al., 2006, Archives of
Internal Medicine 166(10):1092-1097) and PHQ-9 (Kroenke, Spitzer, Williams,
2001, Journal of General Internal Medicine 16(9):606-613). Originally
developed with Pfizer educational grant support; in 2010 Pfizer released
both instruments without copyright restriction — no permission is required
to reproduce, translate, display, or distribute them.
LICENSE_NOTE: Public domain / unrestricted use as of 2010. Verify current
terms at the time of any production deployment, as licensing statements
can be updated.
NON_DIAGNOSTIC_REMINDER: These are screening instruments, not diagnostic
tools. A score above a clinical cutoff indicates a need for further
professional evaluation, not a diagnosis. AnxioSense does not compute or
display a GAD-7/PHQ-9 total score as a diagnostic output — items are used
only as citable, validated indicator language.

---

CHUNK_ID: INST-GAD7-INTRO
INSTRUMENT: GAD-7
SOURCE_TYPE: validated_instrument_item
CONTENT: Over the last two weeks, how often have you been bothered by the
following problems? Response options: Not at all (0), Several days (1),
More than half the days (2), Nearly every day (3).
SCORING_NOTE: Total score range 0-21. Cutoffs: 5 = mild, 10 = moderate,
15 = severe. A score of 10 or greater is the standard threshold suggesting
further evaluation is warranted. AnxioSense does not apply this scoring
directly to free-text input; included for reference only.

CHUNK_ID: INST-PHQ9-INTRO
INSTRUMENT: PHQ-9
SOURCE_TYPE: validated_instrument_item
CONTENT: Over the last two weeks, how often have you been bothered by any
of the following problems? Response options: Not at all (0), Several days
(1), More than half the days (2), Nearly every day (3).
SCORING_NOTE: Total score range 0-27. Cutoffs: 5 = mild, 10 = moderate,
15 = moderately severe, 20 = severe. AnxioSense does not apply this
scoring directly to free-text input; included for reference only.
SAFETY_NOTE: PHQ-9 item 9 concerns self-harm and suicidal ideation. In
AnxioSense, this item is NOT used by the Retrieval or Validation agents
for general symptom matching. It exists here only as part of the
instrument's documented structure. Any user content suggestive of
self-harm or suicidal ideation must be handled exclusively by the
Safety/Crisis Check gate and safety_boundary.txt — never surfaced as a
retrieved "symptom indicator" in a standard report.
