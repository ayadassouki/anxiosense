KB_FILE: depression_indicators.txt
PURPOSE: Depression-specific clinical indicators for retrieval and
claim-evidence matching. Source: World Health Organization, ICD-11
(2025-01 release), category 6A70 Single Episode Depressive Disorder /
Depressive Episode. Used under CC BY-ND 3.0 IGO
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/3.0/igo/).
NON_DIAGNOSTIC_REMINDER: These entries describe symptom patterns, not
diagnoses. AnxioSense does not assess or report on suicidal ideation as a
"symptom indicator" — any such content is routed exclusively through the
Safety/Crisis Check gate and safety_boundary.txt, never through this file.

---

CHUNK_ID: DEP-001
INDICATOR: Depressed Mood
SOURCE: ICD-11 6A70, WHO (2025-01)
SOURCE_TYPE: clinical_description
DESCRIPTION: A period of depressed mood occurring most of the day, nearly
every day, for at least two weeks — experienced as persistent sadness,
emptiness, or a heavy, low feeling.
PLAIN_LANGUAGE_EXAMPLES: "I just feel empty most of the time." "There's
this heaviness that doesn't lift, even on good days."
NON_DIAGNOSTIC_NOTE: Low mood is a normal human experience, especially in
response to difficult events. This indicator describes mood that is
persistent (most of most days) and sustained (at least two weeks), not a
brief or situational dip.

---

CHUNK_ID: DEP-002
INDICATOR: Anhedonia (Diminished Interest or Pleasure)
SOURCE: ICD-11 6A70, WHO (2025-01)
SOURCE_TYPE: clinical_description
DESCRIPTION: Markedly diminished interest or pleasure in activities that
were previously enjoyable, occurring most of the day, nearly every day,
for at least two weeks.
PLAIN_LANGUAGE_EXAMPLES: "Nothing really feels enjoyable anymore, not even
things I used to look forward to." "I don't care about hanging out with
friends the way I used to."
NON_DIAGNOSTIC_NOTE: This is one of the two core gateway features of a
depressive episode under ICD-11 (alongside depressed mood) and is
considered relatively specific to depressive presentations rather than
anxiety — see anxiety_vs_depression.txt.

---

CHUNK_ID: DEP-003
INDICATOR: Reduced Energy / Fatigue (Depression Presentation)
SOURCE: ICD-11 6A70, WHO (2025-01)
SOURCE_TYPE: clinical_description
DESCRIPTION: Reduced energy or persistent fatigue, often described as
effortful even for routine tasks, not substantially relieved by rest.
PLAIN_LANGUAGE_EXAMPLES: "Even getting out of bed feels like a huge
effort." "I'm tired no matter how much I sleep."
NON_DIAGNOSTIC_NOTE: Fatigue is a shared symptom across many conditions,
including anxiety, and has many non-psychiatric causes (e.g., anemia,
thyroid conditions, sleep disorders). See shared_symptoms.txt.

---

CHUNK_ID: DEP-004
INDICATOR: Feelings of Worthlessness or Excessive Guilt
SOURCE: ICD-11 6A70, WHO (2025-01)
SOURCE_TYPE: clinical_description
DESCRIPTION: Persistent feelings of worthlessness, or excessive or
inappropriate guilt, extending beyond reasonable self-criticism about a
specific event.
PLAIN_LANGUAGE_EXAMPLES: "I feel like a burden to everyone around me."
"Everything that goes wrong feels like it's my fault, even when it
isn't."
NON_DIAGNOSTIC_NOTE: Relevant to referral_guidelines.txt severity tiering.
Profound hopelessness combined with this indicator should be cross-checked
against safety_boundary.txt escalation criteria.

---

CHUNK_ID: DEP-005
INDICATOR: Difficulty Concentrating (Depression Presentation)
SOURCE: ICD-11 6A70, WHO (2025-01)
SOURCE_TYPE: clinical_description
DESCRIPTION: Difficulty concentrating or making decisions, often described
alongside mental slowing or low motivation rather than active worry.
PLAIN_LANGUAGE_EXAMPLES: "I can't make even small decisions anymore." "My
thinking feels slow and foggy."
NON_DIAGNOSTIC_NOTE: Compare to ANX-004 in anxiety_indicators.txt — the
underlying mechanism (worry-driven vs. energy/motivation-driven) differs
even though the surface symptom looks similar. See shared_symptoms.txt.

---

CHUNK_ID: DEP-006
INDICATOR: Sleep and Appetite Changes (Depression Presentation)
SOURCE: ICD-11 6A70, WHO (2025-01)
SOURCE_TYPE: clinical_description
DESCRIPTION: Changes in sleep (commonly early-morning waking or
hypersomnia) or appetite (increase or decrease), occurring alongside other
depressive features.
PLAIN_LANGUAGE_EXAMPLES: "I wake up at 4am and can't get back to sleep."
"I have no interest in food most days." / "I've been eating a lot more
than usual without really noticing."
NON_DIAGNOSTIC_NOTE: See anxiety_vs_depression.txt for how sleep-pattern
type (onset vs. early-waking) helps differentiate anxiety from depression
presentations.

---

CHUNK_ID: DEP-007
INDICATOR: Psychomotor Agitation or Retardation
SOURCE: ICD-11 6A70, WHO (2025-01)
SOURCE_TYPE: clinical_description
DESCRIPTION: Observable slowing of movement and speech (retardation), or
its opposite, restless agitation, occurring alongside other depressive
features.
PLAIN_LANGUAGE_EXAMPLES: "I feel like I'm moving in slow motion." /
"I can't stop pacing, even though I feel exhausted."
NON_DIAGNOSTIC_NOTE: Agitated presentations of depression can closely
resemble anxiety. See anxiety_vs_depression.txt, "Difficult
Differentiation Situations."
